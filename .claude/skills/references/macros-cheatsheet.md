## Frying oil absorption (when estimating fried dishes)

Frying adds significant fat and calories. Use these estimates:

| Food | Oil absorbed per 100g of food |
|---|---|
| Eggplant (sliced) | 20-30g (it soaks up enormously) |
| Potatoes (chips/fries) | 8-12g |
| Breaded items (cutlets, fritters) | 10-15g |
| Battered fish | 12-18g |

At 880 kcal per 100ml, that's roughly **270 kcal of added fat per 30g of absorbed oil**. This is often the single biggest factor in a fried dish's macros — don't skip it.

## Quick mental math

When summing across a recipe, this shortcut helps:

- **Protein** comes mainly from one or two big-ticket ingredients (meat, fish, dairy, legumes). Estimate those first.
- **Carbs** come mainly from grains, pasta, bread, sugar, starchy veg. Estimate those next.
- **Fat** comes from added oils + cheese + fatty meats + nuts. Don't forget the cooking oil — even a "splash" is 5-10g.

Verify with the formula: `protein×4 + carbs×4 + fat×9 ≈ kcal`. If your numbers are >15% off this formula, something is miscounted.
